Reading the map

Each point is one USDA-confirmed detection, placed at its county centroid. Colour is the severity tier FlockGuard would assign given the poultry processing facilities in and around that county — recency and proximity, not case count.

Shaded regions are the four administrative waterfowl flyways. Migration phase in each popup is the continental calendar window for that date.

Detection data: USDA APHIS · Flyway boundaries: USFWS · Updated —
This map displays publicly available USDA APHIS confirmed HPAI detections. Locations are shown at the county centroid — exact farm locations are not published by USDA. Data updates with each scheduled pull.
