Method

From state quarantine to your inbox — here's the gap we close.

When a state animal health official quarantines a farm, USDA typically confirms it 2 to 6 days later. In that window, your procurement team is flying blind. FlockGuard monitors state disclosures and alerts you within hours — before the USDA dashboard updates, and only for the farms you actually buy from.

Onboarding takes a few days. After that, nothing.

Detection alerts arrive within the hour. A weekly risk note lands every Monday. You don't log in, check a dashboard, or remember to do anything.

01

We map your actual farms — by name, not by county

Send us your supplier list in whatever form you keep it — a spreadsheet, an email, a purchase-order export. We map each farm precisely and send the confirmed list back before we go live.

A wrong farm location produces a wrong alert. We'd rather take a day to get it right than send you noise for a year.

02

We watch what state officials publish — before USDA does

State animal health authorities publish quarantine notices days before USDA confirms them. We monitor those alongside federal APHIS reports and control-zone orders around the clock.

Every alert we send includes the original source, so you can verify it yourself in one click.

What we don't do

We're a reading of the public record, not an authority on it.

—We don't test flocks or confirm detections. We read and correlate what public authorities publish.
—We don't tell you what to do with your operation — that's your call. We tell you what's near your farms and what it's likely to mean.
—We never share your supplier list. We sign an NDA before you send us anything.
